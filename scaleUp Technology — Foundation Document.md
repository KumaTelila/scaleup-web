## Vision

To be the technology partner that African organizations trust to turn ambitious operations into systems that scale.

In ten years, an organization of any size in our region should be able to run on reliable, well-designed software built by people who understand its context, not on imported tools that were never made for it.

## Mission

We design, build and run business software and IT infrastructure that remove operational friction for growing companies, institutions and public-sector bodies. We deliver it with engineering discipline, clear communication and long-term support, so clients can grow without rebuilding their systems every two years.

## Core values

1. **Built to scale.** We choose architecture and process that still work at ten times today's volume.
2. **Local context first.** We design for how organizations here actually operate: language, regulation, payments, connectivity.
3. **Own the outcome.** We are accountable for results in production, not for tickets closed.
4. **Straight talk.** We state risks, delays and trade-offs early, in plain words.
5. **Craft over shortcuts.** Documented, tested, maintainable work is the default, including under deadline pressure.
6. **Grow people.** We invest in training and give people real responsibility early.

## The problem we solve

Growing organizations outgrow spreadsheets and disconnected tools long before they can afford a large in-house engineering team. They face three recurring gaps:

- **Fragmented operations.** Sales, inventory, finance, HR and service run in separate tools, so leaders cannot see one version of the truth.
- **Poor fit.** Imported software ignores local invoicing rules, payment channels, languages and approval chains, and customizing it is slow and expensive.
- **Fragile delivery.** Projects stall because vendors under-document, change staff or disappear after go-live, leaving the client dependent and exposed.

## Delivery model and engineering principles

Every engagement follows the same five stages, so clients know what happens next.

1. **Discover.** Interviews, process mapping and a written requirements specification signed off by the client.
2. **Design.** Solution architecture, data model, integration plan and a fixed-scope estimate.
3. **Build.** Short iterations with a demo at the end of each, tracked in a shared backlog.
4. **Launch.** Data migration, user training, a rehearsed cut-over and a rollback plan.
5. **Run.** Monitoring, backups, upgrades and a support agreement with response times.

Engineering principles: code review on every change, automated tests on critical flows, separate development, staging and production environments, version control for everything, and written documentation delivered with every release.

## Business model

Revenue should shift over time from one-off projects toward recurring income, which makes the company more stable and more valuable.

| Stream | How we charge | Role in the model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Implementation, development and consulting projects | Fixed price per phase, milestone-based invoicing | Cash engine in year one |
| Support, managed services and infrastructure | Monthly retainer with defined response times | Recurring base, target majority of revenue |
| Dedicated team / staff augmentation | Monthly rate per engineer | Fills capacity, funds product work |
| Own products | Subscription or license per business | Long-term upside |

Growth levers: referrals from live clients, a small set of reference case studies, partnerships with consultancies and training providers, and reuse of internal modules to cut delivery time.

## Brand and culture

**Voice:** clear, confident, practical. We explain systems in the client's words and avoid jargon.

**Identity:** the name points to growth, so visuals should feel precise and modern, not flashy. Capitalization stays "ScaleUp Tech PLC" in every document and on every channel.

**Culture:** a small, senior-leaning team that reviews each other's work, shares knowledge openly, and treats every client system as if we will be maintaining it for ten years. Weekly team sync, monthly retrospectives, and a written record of decisions.
